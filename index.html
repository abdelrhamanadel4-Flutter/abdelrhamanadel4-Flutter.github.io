About Me

I'm a 4th-year Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Luxor University. I started my Flutter journey through structured training at Route Academy, where I built a solid foundation in Dart, Flutter, and modern mobile development practices.

Beyond mobile development, I completed a UI/UX Design training at NTI, which gave me a stronger eye for user-centered design and interface quality. I'm genuinely interested in building applications that solve real problems and feel good to use.

I work with Clean Architecture, BLoC/Cubit, Provider, and Riverpod for state management. I'm comfortable integrating Firebase services, consuming REST APIs with Dio, and exploring WebSocket for real-time features. I care about writing maintainable, well-structured code.
